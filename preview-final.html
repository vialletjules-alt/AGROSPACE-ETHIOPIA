Ethiopia is the largest coffee producer in Africa and among the top exporters globally. The sector generates roughly $3 billion in export revenue and supports over 15 million people.
The Ethiopia Commodity Exchange is the national trading platform. Prices are discovered through open outcry auctions, then published in ETB per kilogram with USD conversion shown on this dashboard.
